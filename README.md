### about

valdis. i write python, lua and luau, and a bit of node.js when i have to. mostly discord bots and roblox games, plus whatever minigame gets stuck in my head.

most of my projects are raw: if it runs, it ships. the exceptions are bots, servers, and anything i actually care about. those get the full treatment.

i use ai as a tool, not a crutch.
